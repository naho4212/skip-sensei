01  /  What it does

Three engines, one goal

Every kind of interruption, handled automatically — from YouTube's own ads to the sponsor read baked into the video to the ads on every other site you visit.

Skip YouTube ads

Clicks “Skip” the instant it appears and fast-forwards the rest — pre-roll, mid-roll, overlays, and the anti-ad-blocker wall.

Skip sponsor segments

AI reads the transcript to find creator sponsor and self-promo reads — then skips past them. Works on brand-new uploads, no database needed.

Block ads across the web

Filter-list ad & tracker blocking on every site, with optional cookie-notice, social, and popup lists — plus a per-site pause.

02  /  The moat

AI where filter lists can't reach

Crowdsourced ad-block databases can't see a creator's sponsor read or an ad a filter list never learned. Ad Sensei uses AI — free on-device by default, or your own key — to handle exactly those.

✓

Transcript-based sponsor detection

Understands the video's actual content to find paid reads — precision-first, so it never cuts real content.

✓

Self-healing selectors

When YouTube changes its layout and breaks skipping, the AI re-finds the button and caches the fix. No update needed.

✓

Gap-filler for missed ads

Catches ads the filter lists miss on a site, learns them once, and hides them on every return visit.

✓

Reviewed popups & consent

Hides annoyance popups but keeps logins and consent dialogs — and clicks “reject all” on cookie prompts for you.
