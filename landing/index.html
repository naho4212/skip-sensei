What it does

One ad blocker, three layers

Start with web-wide ad and tracker blocking — then add the ads filter lists can't reach: the ones sites serve from their own domains, including YouTube's in-stream video ads.

01

Block ads across the web

Filter-list ad & tracker blocking on every site, with cookie-notice, popup, social, and URL-tracking lists — pick a Balanced or Max level at install, pause any site from the popup.

02

Block first-party ads

The ads sites serve themselves, which filter lists miss — sponsored pins, promoted products, feed ads, and YouTube's in-stream video ads. Hidden or removed automatically.

03

Skip YouTube ads & sponsor reads

On YouTube it also clicks “Skip” the instant it appears and uses AI to skip creator sponsor and self-promo reads — pre-roll, mid-roll, overlays, and the anti-ad-blocker wall.

The moat

AI where filter lists can't reach

Crowdsourced ad-block databases can't see a creator's sponsor read or an ad a filter list never learned. Ad Sensei uses AI — free on-device by default, or your own key — to handle exactly those.

Transcript-based sponsor detection

Understands the video's actual content to find paid reads — precision-first, so it never cuts real content.

Self-healing selectors

When YouTube changes its layout and breaks skipping, the AI re-finds the button and caches the fix. No update needed.

Gap-filler for missed ads

Catches ads the filter lists miss on a site, learns them once, and hides them on every return visit.

Reviewed popups & consent

Hides annoyance popups but keeps logins and consent dialogs — and clicks “reject all” on cookie prompts for you.

Runs on Chrome's built-in on-device model — nothing you watch is sent anywhere

By design

Powerful. Private. Free.

No accounts, no tracking profile. Blocking and analysis run in your browser, not on a server. Optional diagnostics report the domain where ad detection went wrong so we can fix it — one click turns them off, and Local-only mode silences every network call.

100%

Client-side

All blocking, all analysis, all your settings live in your browser. Nothing you watch, type, or read is uploaded — and if our server vanished, blocking would keep working.

$0

Free by default

On-device AI needs no key and no card. Bring a free Gemini or Groq key for extra speed — or run fully local with Ollama.

8

AI providers

Built-in Chrome AI, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, local Ollama, your OpenClaw, Claude, or OpenAI — with automatic fallback to on-device if a key hits its limit.

And more

The details are handled

The small stuff that makes an ad blocker feel invisible — built in, not bolted on.

Anti-adblock wall recovery

If YouTube throws its ad-blocker wall, Ad Sensei backs off, clears the right cookies, and gets playback going again.

Filters that stay fresh

Filter lists refresh themselves between releases — verified, data-only updates, no waiting on a new version.

Per-site control

Pause everything on one site, or tune popup, cookie, and social blocking site-by-site from the popup.

No ad flash

The skip engine runs before YouTube's player finishes loading and clicks “Skip” the same instant it renders.

Honest stats

Session and all-time counts of what was actually skipped and blocked — real ad slots, not inflated request tallies.

Light on permissions

Runs with minimal site access out of the box — broader access stays optional, granted only if you choose.

Get started

Three steps. One minute.

No account, no sign-in. Download the build and load it into Chrome — that's the whole setup.

1

Download

Grab the extension with Add to Chrome and unzip it anywhere on your computer.

2

Load it in

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and click Load unpacked → pick the folder.

3

Pick your level

The welcome page opens on install — choose Essential, Balanced, or Max blocking (and optionally add free AI keys). Then just open YouTube: skipping starts automatically.
