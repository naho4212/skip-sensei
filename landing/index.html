What it does

One ad blocker, three layers

Start with web-wide ad and tracker blocking — then add the ads filter lists can't reach: the ones sites serve from their own domains, including YouTube's in-stream video ads.

01

Block ads across the web

Filter-list ad & tracker blocking on every site, with cookie-notice, popup, social, and URL-tracking lists — pick a Balanced or Max level at install, pause any site from the popup.

02

Block first-party ads

The ads sites serve themselves, which filter lists miss — sponsored pins, promoted products, feed ads, and YouTube's in-stream video ads. Hidden or removed automatically.

03

Skip YouTube ads & sponsor reads

On YouTube it also clicks “Skip” the instant it appears and uses AI to skip creator sponsor and self-promo reads — pre-roll, mid-roll, overlays, and the anti-ad-blocker wall.

Where it works

Every surface, covered honestly

Different surfaces need different tactics — and some have hard limits no extension can cross. Here's exactly what happens where.

Web

Every website

  • Ads, banners & popups blocked by filter lists before they load
  • Trackers and tracking links stripped as you browse
  • Cookie prompts hidden — “reject all” clicked for you
  • “Disable your ad blocker” walls defused, with one-tap recovery when a site digs in
  • First-party ads — sponsored pins, promoted posts, feed ads — hidden by AI where lists can't see them
YouTube

YouTube & YouTube Music

  • In-stream video ads skipped the instant they start — pre-roll, mid-roll, and multi-ad breaks
  • Creator sponsor reads AI-detected from the transcript and skipped, even on brand-new uploads
  • Feed, sidebar & masthead ads hidden
  • First-party adsbeta — optional: strips ad slots from YouTube's own video data so most never start
  • YouTube Music uses the same player — same skipping, verified
  • Optional tidy-ups: hide Shorts, end cards, and the “Still watching?” prompt
Music

Music streaming

  • SoundCloudblocked — the ad request never leaves your browser
  • Pandorablocked — ads blocked, and no “Ad Blocker is on” wall
  • Spotifymuted — ads are silenced by default: the tab is muted for exactly as long as each ad plays
  • Spotify skipbeta — optional: rewrites the web player so ad tracks are skipped outright, no audio at all

Web players only — no extension can reach the desktop or mobile apps. Spotify skipping is experimental: Spotify changes its player often, so muting stays on as the reliable fallback.

Per-site control
