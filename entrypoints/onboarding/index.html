Welcome to Ad Sensei

Three ways to a cleaner, ad-free browse.

  1. Skip YouTube ads

    Automatically skips YouTube's ads — clicks “Skip” the instant it appears and fast-forwards the rest. On by default.

  2. Skip sponsor segments

    Detects creator-read sponsor and self-promo segments by analyzing the video transcript with AI, then skips playback past them — even on brand-new uploads no database has seen. On by default.

  3. Block ads across the web

    Optional filter-list blocking of ads, trackers, cookie banners, popups, and social widgets on every site, with a per-site pause. Off by default — pick a level below.

Pick your blocking level

Prefer to build your own? Pick individual features in settings. You can change level anytime from the popup or settings.

The sponsor-detection AI

Sponsor detection runs on Chrome's built-in AI by default — free, private, on-device, no setup. It's slower on very long videos. For fast, accurate analysis of anything (great for podcasts), add a free Google Gemini API key — no credit card needed. Groq, OpenRouter, and fully-local Ollama are supported too (in settings).

Stored locally, sent only to Google. Chrome will ask to allow access to Google's API host. “Maybe later” leaves a reminder in the toolbar popup.

Your data

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you watch or visit is collected. Anonymous crash reports (error text + a random install ID — never URLs, video titles, or page content) help us fix bugs. They're on by default; turn them off here or in settings anytime.
